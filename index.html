Our Ground Rules

What you can expect from us

Admissions in Kolkata private institutions should be straightforward, honest, and documented. Here is our ethical commitment to your family.

No seat guarantees or false promises

A legitimate seat depends on your rank, the official category rules, and verified seat matrix. We advise extreme caution with anyone who promises backdoor seats.

Total cost in writing before paying a rupee

Before you pay any college deposit, we present tuition, admission fees, semester exams, hostel, and mess charges year-by-year in a written schedule.

The decision always stays with your family

We present accurate facts, placement averages, and options with pros and cons. You and your family decide the course and institution without pressure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions parents & students ask us

Clear answers regarding counselling timelines, seat distribution, and official fee schedules in Kolkata.

When should we begin the admission process?

Class 11 or early Class 12 is ideal for shortlist planning. Check the official WBJEEB, NTA, and WBMCC portals for exact registration and counselling dates each year.

How do direct admission, quotas, and fees work in private colleges?

Seat distribution and fees follow the official notice for the year; we show you the written schedule for your chosen college.

How do State Quota and Management Quota differ in private Medical colleges?

Seat distribution and fees follow the official notice for the year; we show you the written schedule for your chosen college from WBMCC and institutional notices.

Are scholarships available for West Bengal students?

Eligible students can apply for the Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship (SVMCM), West Bengal Free-ship Scheme (WBFS), and Aikyashree / OASIS schemes subject to the government's current eligibility criteria and grant notice.
