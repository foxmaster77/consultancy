Frequently Asked Questions

Questions parents & students ask us

Real answers regarding cutoffs, direct admission rules, and fee structures in Kolkata.

When should we begin the admission process?

Class 11 or early Class 12 is ideal for shortlist planning. Entrance registrations (WBJEE, JEE Main, NEET) happen between December and March. Counselling begins promptly after results.

Can we get direct admission in top colleges like HIT or IEM?

Yes. In West Bengal, private engineering colleges have 80% seats filled through WBJEE counselling, 10% via JEE Main, and 10% through Institute/Management quota strictly adhering to AICTE/MAKAUT norms.

What is the difference between State Quota and Management Quota in private Medical?

In colleges like KPC Medical College or JIMSH, State Quota seats (allotted to domicile students via WBMCC NEET counselling) have government-regulated fees of ~₹4.5L–₹5.5L/year. Management Quota seats range from ₹18L–₹22L/year.

Are scholarships available for West Bengal students?

Yes! Eligible students can avail the Swami Vivekananda Merit-cum-Means Scholarship (SVMCM - ₹60,000/year for engineering), West Bengal Free-ship Scheme (WBFS), and Aikyashree / OASIS for reserved categories.
