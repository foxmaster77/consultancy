Privacy Policy

Chatterjee Corporate Solutions (Admissions Division) · Kolkata

We respect the privacy of students and parents who use this website. This policy explains what information we collect when you submit an enquiry or download our checklist, and how we use it.

1. Information you provide

  • Student or parent name and phone number.
  • Preferred stream, Class 12 board, target college, and any message you choose to write.

2. How we use this information

We use your details solely to respond to your college admissions enquiry, share written fee schedules on request, or send the requested counselling callback. We do not sell, rent, or trade student or parent contact details to external telemarketers or third-party lead brokers.

3. Storage & security

Enquiries submitted through the form are stored in our private database (Supabase) with restricted server-side access. Basic server rate limiting is used to prevent automated spam.

4. Analytics

We use privacy-friendly Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate page visits and tool usage (such as calculator checks, college comparisons, and enquiry submissions) without selling personal profiles.
